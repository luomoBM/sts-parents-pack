Sort the Toys Parents' Pack

Everything a parent needs to know about the free browser tidy game — in five short sections. Verified 2026-11-17.

The game in one line

Grab three identical toys and the shelf clears, against a gentle timer. The game runs in any browser, phone or desktop, with nothing to install and no account to create.

What it costs

Nothing. The family — Shelf Sort and Taba Lapka included — is free and accountless, stated per game on the clean-up hub.

What it teaches

Scanning before acting, counting before moving, and saving resources for real problems — the three habits are a tidy little thinking curriculum disguised as housework.

How sessions end

Timers end rounds naturally; three rounds is a session. No streaks, no guilt mechanics, no purchases to explain — the only paid sibling is a separate Steam PC game, clearly a different product.
